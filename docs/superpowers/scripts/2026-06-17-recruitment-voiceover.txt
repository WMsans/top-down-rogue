# === ACT 1, Scene 1.1: The Destructible World [0:10-0:20] ===
I'm making an action roguelike.
Kinda like Noita — every pixel is part of a physical simulation.
This achieves fully desturctable terrain, and something interesting.

# === ACT 1, Scene 1.2: The Living Elements [0:20-0:30] ===
Gas and liquids flow and pool through the tunnels you carve.
Fire ignites oil, water douses flames, while oil fuels them.
That's why I said its kinda like Noita.

# === ACT 2, Scene 2.1: The Engine [0:30-0:40] ===
Under the hood, it's a GPU compute shader-driven physics simulation.
Each chunk is 256 pixels — essentially a cellular automaton.
Terrain syncs back to the CPU every 4 frames,
so it can do things where pure-GPU approach can't, like lighting effects.

# === ACT 2, Scene 2.2: The Systems [0:40-0:50] ===
The rougelike build system is still in development, but it's roughly based on weapons and modifiers,
with heavy Balatro and Noita influences — though this system will probably get a major overhaul.
Although currently there's about 17,000 lines of GDScript in total, 
there still isn't enough content yet to feel like a real roguelike, only combat and basic enemy AI are done.
