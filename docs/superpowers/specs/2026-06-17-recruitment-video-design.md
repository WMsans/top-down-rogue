# Video Spec: Godot Action-Roguelike Recruitment Video

> Created: 2026-06-17 | Landscape analyzed: 250+ videos across 5 keywords | Approach: "The Physics Engine Has No Art" (hybrid spectacle + honest canvas)

## Core Concept
A 60-90 second recruitment video that opens with a 10-second explosion-meme montage (pure spectacle, no text), then uses text-narrated gameplay footage to explain the game, what's been built, and why creators should join — a hobby project offering real portfolio work and full creative credit.

## Target Audience
Indie-curious creators on Bilibili — primarily 2D artists, then game designers, then programmers, then sound designers/composers. They already know Godot (or want to learn it), have some skill to contribute, and are looking for a real portfolio piece that isn't a 48-hour game jam throwaway. They understand and accept "unpaid hobby project."

## Hook (0:00 - 0:10)
Pure visual, no text or VO. A montage of explosions across different scenes and biomes. Each explosion gets a different meme overlay (brain galaxy, "this is fine" dog, surprised Pikachu, etc.). Music only, building tension into the peak explosion. The hook sells one idea: *this game does something you haven't seen before.*

## Structure Overview

| Time | Section | Visual | Audio |
|------|---------|--------|-------|
| 0:00-0:10 | **Hook** | Explosion montage across scenes/biomes, meme overlays on each | Music only, building |
| 0:10-0:30 | **What is this?** | Text-narrated game intro. Terrain carving, gas flow, fluid pooling, status combos — each mechanic gets its own clip cluster with supporting text overlay | VO begins, music dips |
| 0:30-0:50 | **What's been built?** | Tech + systems depth. GPU simulation footage, CSV weapon table, status reaction matrix. Engine stats: ~17k lines GDScript, data-driven architecture, compute-shader terrain, working combat feel | VO continues |
| 0:50-1:15 | **Why join?** | Hobby project, unpaid, fully credited. Real portfolio piece, full creative ownership over your domain. Brief role call with what each role would own. No hard sell | VO |
| 1:15-1:30 | **CTA** | Discord link, role summary card, final meme. Fade out | Music resolve |

## Approach Rationale
Chosen over alternatives grounded in landscape data:
- **60-90s short form** (vs. 3-8min narrative): Safara's 1:17 video achieved 19.4% like ratio — 6x the recruitment niche average. Short-form recruitment/demo hybrids are nearly empty on Bilibili.
- **Spectacle-first hook** (vs. talking-head or feature-walkthrough): The gas/fluid simulation is the project's strongest differentiator. Leading with explosions is a category-creating move — no existing Godot recruitment video opens this way.
- **Meme-textured tone** (vs. corporate pitch): Matches Bilibili indie dev culture (Safara, 大泽三木). Signals "this is a fun side project" and keeps the recruitment from feeling like a startup ad.
- **Benefits-forward recruitment** (vs. needs-forward): Artists want to know what they'll *get* (portfolio, credit, ownership), not just what the project *needs*.

## Differentiation
1. **Category creation:** Godot tutorials are saturated (906K+ views on top result). Godot *recruitment showcases* barely exist. A Godot game with GPU-simulated terrain destruction and gas propagation is unique on the platform.
2. **Placeholder art as opportunity:** Most devs apologize for placeholder art. This video frames it as "the engine is real, the canvas is blank — your work defines how this looks." No existing Bilibili recruitment video uses this angle.
3. **"Recruitment as reverse devlog":** Most recruitment videos are talking-head pleas with no gameplay. This one opens with spectacle and lets the game sell the project before the text even starts.
4. **Honest about scope:** Explicitly states "unpaid hobby project" — filters out mismatched expectations and attracts people who want craft, not a paycheck.

## Duration Target
75-90 seconds. Justified by Safara's 1:17 (160K views, 19.4% like ratio) and the near-absence of sub-2-minute recruitment/demo hybrids in the landscape.

## Visual Style
- **Primary:** Screen recording (gameplay footage) — no on-camera host
- **Overlays:** Text captions for narration, meme inserts for comic relief
- **Audio:** Voiceover narration (recorded in post), background music throughout
- **Meme usage:** Reactive image overlays timed to explosions and spectacle moments. Used sparingly as texture, not as primary content

## Key Observations from Landscape
- "Recruitment + gameplay demo" combined format is a near-vacuum on Bilibili (low saturation across all 5 searched keywords)
- Short-form (<2min) recruitment videos average 15-25K views but have almost no competition
- Safara's "你管这个叫像素游戏?" proves ultra-short game showcases with meme texture can achieve viral-level engagement (19.4% like ratio)
- 《群》概念先行片 showed that concept-forward recruitment with high production value achieves 13.4% like ratio
- Godot-specific recruitment content is essentially nonexistent — this is a category-creation opportunity
